## About

Clinical informatics specialist with a background in pharmacy. I am completing an M.S. in Health Informatics at Indiana University Indianapolis (December 2026) and work as a Graduate Research Assistant in the PLHI Lab, building EHR-integrated and AI-enabled clinical tools on OpenEMR, OpenMRS and Epic.

**Areas of work**

- **EHR integration:** SMART on FHIR applications, FHIR R4, HL7 v2, CDS Hooks, OpenEMR and OpenMRS modules
- **Automated clinical coding:** LLM-based ICD-10-CM and CPT coding from clinical notes, benchmarked on MDACE and synthetic notes
- **Clinical decision support:** MASLD screening with FIB-4 risk stratification, developed and tested in the Epic sandbox
- **Clinical data:** OMOP CDM, SNOMED CT, LOINC, ICD-10, ETL pipelines and synthetic patient generation with Synthea
